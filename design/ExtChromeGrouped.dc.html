Planner Choice Planner
Browser extension · free · open source

Your plan, on MCC, in one click.

MCC Filler copies your ranked list from the planner into MCC's choice-filling page, or brings MCC's list back. You sign in, save with the OTP and lock by yourself.

v1.3.1 · also for Edge and Brave
Updates itself from the store
  • Never sees your MCC password, security pin or OTP
  • Never presses Lock or final submit
  • Makes no network requests of its own

Ready in three steps

  1. 1 Add it to Chrome Press the Chrome Web Store badge, then Add to Chrome.
  2. 2 Open your plan Reload the planner. Your list syncs to the extension by itself.
  3. 3 Fill MCC On MCC's Choice Filling, press Plan → MCC, check the list, then save with the OTP.
Try it before your MCC session The practice page works like MCC's choice filling with this year's choices. Nothing there reaches MCC.
Open the practice page

Install by hand

For Chrome without the store, or to read the code. A hand-installed copy doesn't update itself: unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and press Load unpacked.
