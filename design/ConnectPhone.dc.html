Set it up in a minute

ChatGPT

  1. Open Settings → Apps. Turn on Developer mode under Advanced settings if asked.
  2. Press Create app, paste your connector link, pick OAuth, and create it.
  3. Sign in with your email when ChatGPT opens the planner, then press Allow.

Claude

  1. Open Settings → Connectors and press Add custom connector.
  2. Paste your connector link, add it, then press Connect.
  3. Sign in with your email, then press Allow.

Other assistant apps

Any assistant app that lets you add a custom connector with sign-in works too. Add your connector link, then sign in with your email and press Allow. Steps vary from app to app.

Tip: open the emailed sign-in link in the same browser that is connecting, so it comes back to the Allow page.
