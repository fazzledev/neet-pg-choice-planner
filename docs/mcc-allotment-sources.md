# Sources for closing ranks

The closing ranks shown in NEET PG Choice Planner are calculated by this tool from the Medical Counselling Committee's published final allotment results below. They are not an official MCC figure. MCC publishes who was allotted which seat; the tool takes the last rank allotted per seat, round, year and category, and matches MCC's institute and course names to the choice list. About 1 in 5 rows could not be matched with confidence and are left out. See `data/cutoffs/match_report.md` for the counts.
